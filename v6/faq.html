Before you install

Why not just use a Google Sheet directly?

You are: that’s the design. What the app adds is a camera, a global product database, a folder view with a count per shelf, a quantity stepper you can hit with a thumb, and columns that arrive already named and formatted. Try typing a 13-digit barcode into a cell on a phone. That is the part we removed.

Do I need a Google account? Can I sign up with an email address?

A Google account is required and there is no alternative: Google Sign-In is the only authentication method the app has. The upside is that signing up takes one tap. There’s no form to fill in and no password to invent, and your first spreadsheet exists before you reach the home screen.

Why does an inventory app need access to my Google Drive?

It asks for drive.file, the narrowest Drive permission Google offers. An app holding it can touch only the files it created itself, so 1inventory can open the spreadsheet and photo folder it made for you and nothing besides. Google enforces that boundary. Gmail, Calendar and the rest of your account stay out of reach.

Will the scanner recognise my things?

Shop-bought goods with retail barcodes come back with a name, description and photo: tins, jars, medicine, ISBN books, board games, Lego, DVDs, batteries. Own-brand and warehouse labels will scan, but the lookup has nothing to fill in. Twelve barcode formats are supported. Lookups are capped at 5 a day on Free and 200 on Premium; scanning itself is unlimited.

Is there a web app? Does it work on an iPad?

No to both. 1inventory is a portrait phone app for iOS 15.6 and later and Android 7.0 and later, with no tablet layout and no browser client. Your data is still usable on a laptop: the spreadsheet opens in Google Sheets like any other file, where you can sort, filter, chart and print it.

What about the list you already keep?

I already have a list. Do I have to retype it?

No. Import a CSV and your column headers are matched to fields by meaning, so a German export headed Bezeichnung / Menge / Lagerort lands in the right columns with no setup. Limits: CSV only, 5 MB, 60 columns, a name column required. The mapping runs in one pass and is shown to you afterwards. Duplicates are skipped.

How many products can I have?

Up to 10,000 per inventory, on the free plan and on Premium alike; the cap has nothing to do with paying. Premium gives you up to five separate inventories, which is 50,000 things in total. The ceiling is enforced: at 10,000 the app returns an error, so you will know you have reached it.

Three things it won’t do

Does it work offline?

No. Every action is a network call, and there is no offline mode. The app labels offline “Coming soon”, which is a plan with no date attached. This matters most in cellars, garages, lock-ups and vans, which are exactly the places an inventory tends to live.

Will it tell me when I’m running low?

No. Nothing in the app watches quantity: there is no reorder point and no par level. What does exist is the date alert. Put an expiry, a warranty end or a service date on a product, and you get a push notification or an email a set number of days before that date, or after it.

What if I need more than five locations or five people?

Then this is probably the wrong product, and we would rather say so now than in a refund email. Five flat places and five people is a household or a small crew, deliberately. Locations don’t nest, there is no read-only role, and per-location permissions don’t exist. None of it is planned.

None of those three is a gap we are about to close. If one of them is a dealbreaker for you, it will still be a dealbreaker in six months, and you should spend your afternoon on a different app.

Letting someone else in

Is it safe to let my partner or a colleague in?

Mostly. They sign in with their own Google account, and where the inventory has a History tab, every change lands there with their name on it. There are two roles and no read-only option, so anyone you invite can delete things. Inviting needs Premium, the cap is five people including you, and the spreadsheet stays in your Drive alone.

Will their changes appear on my phone straight away?

No. Changes reach your Google Sheet when they are saved, but another phone shows them only after someone pulls the list down to refresh. Nothing updates by itself while you are looking at it. Two people counting the same shelf at the same time will overwrite each other’s numbers.

What does Premium cost?

€0.99 a month, with a seven-day free trial. Billing is monthly and there is no annual plan, no bundle and no second tier. The subscription is handled by the App Store or Google Play, so you cancel it there, and the app shows the price in your own currency.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. Extra inventories, locations, custom fields and the people you invited are locked, and all of it comes back in one pass if you resubscribe. Your products are untouched, because they live in your Google Sheet. Be aware that a locked inventory is hidden while it is locked, and the people you invited lose access.

Is it worth paying for?

Probably not yet. Catalogue a room on the free plan first. Within a fortnight you will know whether you have hit one of the five walls: a second inventory, a second place, a second person, a second alert rule, or a change history. If you haven’t hit one, don’t pay us.

If we disappear

Can I export my data?

There is no export button, because there is nothing to export. Your inventory is already a Google Sheet in your own Drive, and Google will save it out as CSV, Excel or PDF whenever you like. One caveat: the file lives in the inventory owner’s Drive, so this is something owners can do and invited people cannot.

What if I delete something by accident?

Because your inventory is a Google Sheet, it inherits Google Drive’s version history. The app’s Backups screen lists the revisions and walks you through restoring one inside Google Sheets. There is no restore button in the app itself. Deleting a custom field removes that whole column from your spreadsheet, values included.

What happens if 1inventory shuts down?

The spreadsheet, its history tab and the photo folder are in your Google Drive and stay there, readable by anything that opens a spreadsheet. What you would lose is the scanner, the barcode lookup and the folder view. We can promise continuity of data, not continuity of service.

Do you show ads?

No, on either plan. There is no advertising SDK in the app, and it has never asked for a tracking identifier or shown you an App Tracking Transparency prompt. The free plan is paid for by the people who upgrade, which is the whole of the business model.

What languages is it in?

English, German, Spanish, French and Portuguese: the whole app, and also the alert emails and the barcode lookup results, which come back in your language. Your language setting belongs to your account, so it follows you when you change phones.
