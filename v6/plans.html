Plans

Pay when one of five things becomes true.

Below is what Premium changes, what Free keeps forever, and what happens to your spreadsheet on the day you stop paying for either.

€0.99 a month for Premium, billed monthly. The first seven days are free.

When does Free stop being enough?

There is a short list of moments. Until one of them arrives there is nothing here for you to buy.

  • You want a second inventory. The pantry and the workshop shouldn’t share a list.
  • You want a second place. A garage as well as a larder.
  • You want a second person: your partner, a colleague, the other person on the committee.
  • You want more than one date watched for you.
  • You want a record of who changed what.

If none of those is true, stay on Free. It isn’t a countdown and it doesn’t expire.

There is no card to enter and no account to create. Stop paying at any point and nothing is deleted; the spreadsheet stays in your Drive either way.

Get the app

History is Premium and it starts on the day you subscribe, so upgrading an inventory you already have doesn’t fill in its past. The 200 daily lookups belong to the inventory and everyone in it draws from the same pool; they reset at 23:59:59 UTC, which is unlikely to be your midnight.

Alert rules watch Date and Date/Time fields you added yourself, so the field comes first. They don’t watch quantity, and there is no low-stock alert in this app. €0.99 is the euro price; the App Store and Google Play show the equivalent in your own currency, and both of them handle the billing, the trial and the cancelling.

What the Premium numbers buy

  • Five inventories. The pantry, the workshop, the books, the band’s gear and the holiday let, each with its own spreadsheet, its own fields and its own icon from a set of 88.
  • Five locations. The garage, the loft, the shed, the stockroom and the van, each with its own folder and its own count.
  • Five people. You and four others, each signing in as themselves, each with their name against every change they make.
  • Fifteen fields of your own. Expiry, warranty end, serial number, edition, condition, purchase price, who you lent it to.
  • 200 barcode lookups a day. Five is a shelf. Two hundred is a room in one sitting.
  • Change history. Every re-count and every deletion, in a tab of your own spreadsheet, with a name against it.

You can see which wall you’re near

If you own the inventory, the app keeps a Dashboard screen in the menu: how many locations, fields, alert rules and people you have used out of your cap, with the Premium figure beside each one. Nothing about the limits is a surprise you meet at the till.

Free is a real plan, not a trial.

Barcode scanning is unlimited and permanent. Up to 10,000 products. CSV import, identical to the version Premium gets. One location, one custom field, and one alert rule with both push and email working properly on it. Your own Google Sheet in your own Drive, with every tab except History.

No ads, on either plan. There is no ad SDK in this app and it has never asked your phone for a tracking identifier.

Stop paying. Delete the app. Outlive us.

If you stop paying

Nothing is deleted. Your extra inventories, locations, fields and teammates are locked. They are still there, and every one of them comes back in a single pass on the day you resubscribe. Your products are untouched throughout, because they were never sitting on our servers to begin with.

Two things you should hear from us first. A locked inventory is hidden while it stays locked, so you can see that it exists and you can’t open it. And anyone you invited loses it from their app until you subscribe again.

If you delete the app, or your 1inventory account

The spreadsheet is in your Drive tonight, and next year, and after you have changed phones twice. Deleting your account offers you a checkbox that wipes the sheets from your Drive as well, and it starts unticked. You would have to go looking for it.

If we put the price up, or get bought, or go under

Sparkybit is a small company in Paderborn and we are not going to pretend that is the same thing as a guarantee. So here is the guarantee that doesn’t depend on us being here. Tomorrow morning your spreadsheet is exactly where it was tonight, with every product, every count, every column you added and every change anybody logged, in the most portable format there is, readable by software owned by somebody else entirely.

What you would lose is the app itself — the scanner, and the lookup that fills a product in for you. The rows stay where they are.

The app makes this argument to itself, as it happens. When it crashes it doesn’t show you a stack trace. It shows you a sentence: “Don’t worry, your data is safe in your Google Sheets.”

We’d rather compete on the footing where you can leave.

Before you pay, read what it doesn’t do.

There is no web app, no desktop app and no offline mode, and it will never tell you that you are running low on something. The full list is on what it doesn’t do, and the awkward questions are answered on the FAQ.

A week is enough to know

Install it, sign in with Google, and catalogue one shelf. If you want a second place or a second person after that, Premium is €0.99 a month and the first seven days cost nothing.
