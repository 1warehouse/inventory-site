Multi-users

Whether you want to grant access to your personal inventory, friends and family, or share it with your warehouse workers as part of your business operations, 1Inventory has all the features you need. Our multi-user access support ensures seamless collaboration and efficient management of your inventory.

Business Ready

Everything a growing operation needs

Barcode Scanning

The application includes a barcode scanning feature that allows quick identification and processing of items, significantly speeding up inventory management tasks such as stock-taking and product relocation.

Hierarchical Location Management

The system allows the organization of inventory across multiple locations, with the ability to set hierarchical relationships between locations, helping manage stock efficiently.

Customisation

Extend how you want, customise where you need!

1Inventory empowers you to tailor your inventory data and mobile app interface to meet your business’s unique requirements. Easily add custom fields, capture specialised information, and adapt the app to your specific industry needs.

Extensive Customization Options

Custom fields for both products and operations in the mobile application, allowing for detailed data collection that supports specific business processes and requirements.

Support for Various Data Types

The system can handle different types, including text, numbers, checkboxes, dropdown lists and date/time entries, making it versatile for any kind of data tracking needed.

Validation for Required Fields

Ensure data completeness and accuracy by setting validation rules for required fields, helping to maintain the integrity of your inventory data and reduce errors in data entry.

Centralized User Setup

Users can be added, activated, or deactivated directly from the Google Sheets interface, allowing administrators to easily manage access to the mobile app. Only a unique PIN or email is required for secure login.

Secure Authorization Mechanism

The application supports secure user authentication, including personalized login links with QR codes or OTP provided by email, ensuring that only authorized personnel can access the app and perform inventory tasks.

Audit Trails and Transaction History

All user actions are logged with details of transactions performed, providing a clear audit trail for accountability and traceability in inventory management.
